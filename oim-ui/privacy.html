Privacy Notice Template

This is a starter page for a self-hosted software project. It is not a completed privacy notice and makes no promise about how a particular deployment processes data.

Before production use, the operator must document the data collected by its deployment, purposes and legal bases, service providers, retention periods, security controls, user rights, and a valid contact method. Call audio, transcripts, caller identifiers, appointment details, and integration tokens may be processed when the related features are enabled.

Replace this page with a notice reviewed for the laws and practices that apply to your service.
